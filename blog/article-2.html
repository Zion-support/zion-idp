The hidden cost of manual processing

Manual invoice processing costs $5-15 per invoice when you factor in data entry, verification, approval routing, and error correction. For a company processing 1,000 invoices/month, that's $60,000-$180,000 annually — before accounting for late payment fees and missed early payment discounts.

AI-powered invoice automation

AI-driven invoice processing reduces per-invoice cost by 80-90% while improving accuracy to 99%+. Key savings come from:

  • Eliminating manual data extraction (saves 5-10 minutes per invoice)
  • Automated matching against POs and receipts
  • Exception-based routing (only exceptions need human review)
  • Faster processing enabling early payment discounts

Calculate your ROI

  • Early payment discounts: 1-2% of invoice value
  • Error reduction: 90% fewer payment errors
