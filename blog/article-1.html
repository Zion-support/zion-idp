IDP 2.0: Why Template-Based OCR Is Dead

LLM-based extraction handles unseen document formats without retraining — and when traditional OCR still wins.

The problem with templates

Traditional OCR systems rely on templates — predefined zones where specific data fields appear. When a document format changes (a new invoice layout, a different contract template), the template breaks and must be manually updated. For organizations processing hundreds of document formats, this approach doesn't scale.

How LLMs changed the game

Modern IDP systems use large language models that understand document structure semantically, not spatially. They can identify an invoice number, a total amount, or a contract date regardless of where it appears on the page — even in documents they've never seen before.

When traditional OCR still wins

Legacy OCR remains superior for:

  • High-volume, fixed-format documents (utility bills, standard forms)
  • Processing speed requirements (millions of documents per hour)
  • Cost-sensitive operations where LLM inference costs would be prohibitive

The future is hybrid: traditional OCR for speed and cost, LLMs for flexibility and accuracy on complex documents.
