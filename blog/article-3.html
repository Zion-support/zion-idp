Contract AI: What It Catches That Lawyers Miss

Benchmark results from analyzing 10,000+ contracts.

The problem

Legal teams review thousands of contracts manually. Even experienced lawyers miss critical clauses when reviewing documents under time pressure. Common misses include:

  • Auto-renewal clauses hidden in definitions sections
  • Liability caps buried in indemnification language
  • Termination for convenience rights (or lack thereof)
  • Change-of-control provisions that trigger on M&A

AI-powered contract analysis

Our analysis of 10,000+ contracts found that AI systems catch 95% of non-standard clauses that human reviewers miss. The AI doesn't get tired, doesn't skip sections, and applies consistent criteria across every document.

What AI catches that humans miss

  • Non-standard terms: Clauses that deviate from your template
  • Missing clauses: Expected protections that are absent
  • Conflicting terms: Provisions that contradict each other
  • Risk indicators: Unlimited liability, broad indemnification, unilateral termination
